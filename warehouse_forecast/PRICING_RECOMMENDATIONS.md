# PoC Pricing Structure - Recommendations

## Problem با Current Pricing

**Current:** $50/hr × 20-30 hours = $1,000-1,500  
**Issue:** Виглядає дешево для senior AI work + part-time має бути дорожче

---

## Recommended Options

### ⭐ Option 1: Fixed Price Pilot (BEST for PoC)

**PoC Investment: $2,500 fixed**

**Payment:**
- 40% ($1,000) upfront
- 60% ($1,500) upon delivery

**Чому добре:**
- ✅ Клієнт знає exact cost
- ✅ Виглядає професійно
- ✅ Не "cheap hourly"
- ✅ Покриває 25-30 hours @ $83-100/hr effective

**Pitch:**
> "1-week PoC Pilot: $2,500 fixed price. Includes complete forecasting system, accuracy validation, dashboard, and ROI projection. Payment: 40% upfront, 60% on delivery."

---

### Option 2: Hybrid Model (Good for Trust-Building)

**Setup Fee: $1,200**
- Architecture + data integration
- Model setup
- First prototype

**Then: $90/hour for refinements**
- Estimated 10-15 additionalчасів
- Total: $2,100-2,550

**Чому добре:**
- ✅ Immediate value показ
- ✅ Flexibility для scope
- ✅ Lower upfront ($1,200 vs $2,500)

**Pitch:**
> "Setup fee $1,200 covers architecture and first prototype (2-3 days). Then $90/hr for refinements. Estimated 10-15 hours. Total ~$2,100-2,500."

---

### Option 3: Premium Hourly (Transparent but Higher)

**Rate: $90/hour** (part-time premium)  
**Estimate:** 20-30 hours  
**Total:** $1,800-2,700

**Rate justification:**
- Part-time context switching overhead
- Senior AI expertise  
- NDA-compliant data handling

**Pitch:**
> "Part-time rate $90/hr (20-30 hours estimated). Reflects context-switching overhead and senior expertise. Total $1,800-2,700."
